## Kept

I kept the linear waveform design.

Each line represents one week of weather data. The length of the line shows the total rainfall, and the colour shows the average temperature.

I used light red for lower temperatures and dark red for higher temperatures.

I chose this design because it is simple and clear. It shows how rainfall and temperature change throughout the year.
